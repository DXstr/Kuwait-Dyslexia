# Basira Noor artistic assets

These static illustrations implement the user-approved Basira purple/gold/cream Noor visual direction. The welcome, listening, teaching and reading poses are the matching generated drafts carried into this UI pass. The celebrating, encouraging and curious poses, gold eye symbol and Kuwait scenery were generated with GPT Image 2.5 through Manus image-generation tools on 2026-11-05, using approved brand/Noor references. They are generated artistic output, not clinical exercise items, diagnostic imagery, anatomical mouth targets or scoring evidence. No child names, recordings or results were included in generation prompts, and the shipped app makes no runtime image/model call.

Native-alpha PNG sources were resized proportionally and encoded as WebP for the standalone app; no character/text content was drawn or added by a script. The original generated sources remain outside the repository. Relevant provider output terms and the IBM Plex font licence are disclosed in `docs/DISCLOSURES.md`; the official IBM Plex Sans Arabic OFL text is shipped at `web/public/fonts/ibm-plex-sans-arabic/OFL.txt`. Existing Noto Naskh Arabic remains the fully vocalized reading/report font. No Duolingo code, mascot, branding or artwork was used.
